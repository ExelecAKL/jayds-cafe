## Pickup and delivery correction

The Customer API already required Maya for delivery, and its authorized Orders detail exposed a pickup capability only for a paid, ready pickup. Cashier's Ready action incorrectly used the app source/account ID to select scanning without checking fulfillment type. That is why a delivery received **Scan pickup**. Its scan action also opened manual entry before opening the camera.

Cards and details identify fulfillment explicitly. Ready mobile pickup, including a guest pickup, opens the scanner immediately. Manual entry is an explicit scanner fallback; denied permission, a missing device and insecure LAN HTTP have separate recovery messages. Camera close stops its tracks. The order QR is in Customer **Orders → open the ready pickup**; it is separate from the loyalty/account QR. Maya pickups require verified prepayment. ASAP mobile cash remains **Pay at pickup** until the scanned cash handover completes payment and collection together.

Ready delivery opens **Book rider**, then **Delivery status / Refresh delivery** and the checked Lalamove tracking link and rider details. The new Cashier server bridge forwards the approved staff member's own session to the existing protected quotation/attempt/dispatch/status endpoints. It preserves quotation-price checks, idempotency and uncertain-attempt reconciliation. Booking does not mark an order delivered: the checked courier synchronization remains responsible for completion and loyalty.

Migrations `20261007000400` and `20261007000500` prevent delivery tokens or counter collection from completing delivery and prevent guest mobile pickup from bypassing its QR. Both were applied to the connected database with ownership/grant and nine-table business-record hash preservation checks. The reported ORD-0055 was already cancelled at preflight; this correction did not book, complete, cancel or otherwise rewrite that live order.

Browser QA used isolated delivery booking/status fixtures and Chromium's fake webcam on localhost, not the laptop's physical camera. It verified the separate cards and actions, booking followed by tracking, pickup/manual collection, webcam startup on the first click, shutdown and permission denial. No live rider booking was issued during this correction. The existing Lalamove sandbox API verification below is a separate earlier provider check. Live camera use on the laptop is available at `http://localhost:4001/dashboard`; LAN HTTP is not a secure camera context. [Browser camera requirements](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/MediaDevices/getUserMedia)

### Ledger demonstration

| Stage                                 | Points | Physical stock | Reserved stock | Recorded spending |
| ------------------------------------- | -----: | -------------: | -------------: | ----------------: |
| Opening                               |    100 |           10 g |            0 g |                ₱0 |
| Submit ₱89 checkout                   |     90 |           10 g |            2 g |                ₱0 |
| Verified settlement fixture           |     90 |           10 g |            2 g |                ₱0 |
| Prepare, including duplicate retry    |     90 |            8 g |            0 g |                ₱0 |
| Collect, including duplicate QR retry |     98 |            8 g |            0 g |               ₱89 |

Admin revenue is **₱189**, not doubled. The cancelled checkout returns its point/voucher usage and unused stock reservation once. Prepared consumption is retained where required by the void/cancellation rules.

## Proposed direction

A compact, product-led phone ordering app informed by ZUS's documented ordering/rewards model, keeping Jayds' own artwork/fonts/colors. Secondary borrowing is limited to Starbucks's ordering sequence. This is a proposed Jayds direction, not a reconstruction of ZUS's native screens.

Keep espresso actions, warm light surfaces, original mascot/banner, Home/Menu/Orders/Account, 48 px primary targets and current transaction safeguards. Avoid a dominant oversized marketing hero, identical heavy cards, stretched/rotated logos, generic pictures presented as drinks and decorative animation. Start at 390 px, verify 360/430 px and keyboards, then check accepted tablet/landscape screens for layout drift.

The supplied screenshots resolve the previous request for recorded visual evidence. Screen layouts and hierarchy are adapted to Jayds; competitor artwork, personal information and commercial offers are not reused.

The broader portrait redesign uses the supplied references and the existing original logo. The portrait layer applies below 768 px, with a narrow-screen keyboard exception. Tablet and normal landscape use the established layout. Home adds compact rewards/favorites, original scalable pickup/delivery illustrations and direct fulfillment entry. Menu uses a category rail, open products, global search and persisted browsing context. Detail shows selected options and total above its fixed action. Checkout retains review and explicit submission with visible recovery errors. Account rows and mobile titles are simplified.

Original artwork: `public/illustrations/jayds-pickup.svg` and `jayds-delivery.svg`, both scalable vectors. Eight new line icons extend the existing 24-unit icon system: cup, leaf, cake, category grid, pastry, chocolate, cookie and food. Real product images retain their aspect ratios; category line art is used where café photography is missing. The live read-only check found 52 published items and zero product photos; photography remains a visible catalog content gap.

Verified logo correction: customer build and focused lint passed; browser checks at 360/390/430/768/1024 px confirm the original image aspect ratio, stable carousel height, 48 px controls, keyboard focus and reduced motion, with zero runtime errors. The real-worker offline reload/draft check also passed with the original logo precached. Tablet/landscape geometry was retained. This validates the asset correction, not a completed broader mobile redesign.

Verified portrait implementation: all three app builds, workspace typecheck and changed-file lint pass. The customer browser suite covers 55 isolated products at 360/390/430/768/834/1024/1280 px, category return state, global search, customization price/quantity/notes, fixed actions at 390×440, saved drafts, stock conflicts, lost-response recovery (three requests, one canonical sale), reorder, GPS denial and reconnect. Motion checks confirm the original logo proportions, 48 px carousel targets, stable slides, keyboard focus and reduced motion. The real-worker check confirms public-only caching, offline reload/editing and retained drafts with 98 public resources. Local fixture timings are not field performance measurements. Live read-only checks confirm 52 published products and HTTP 200 for the LAN preview, manifest and both illustrations. No live orders, provider bookings or private records were changed.

The offline test now releases its fixture server before closing Chromium; a stalled worker teardown had retained an older build between runs. The customer test waits for reconnect/navigation recovery and verifies its offline addition survives before collecting metrics. Both suites passed against the final fresh build. The current preview runs at `http://192.168.18.7:4000/menu`.

## 7 October fixes and verification

Reproduced both reported LAN HTTP failures. Product configuration and authenticated order subscriptions used `crypto.randomUUID`, which is unavailable on insecure Wi-Fi origins. A shared UUID helper now uses the browser's cryptographic random bytes when the native UUID API is absent. Customer, cashier and administrator callers use the same helper; blocked session storage preserves the original checkout retry key and immutable payload in memory. Login catches connection failures, retains entries and clears its pending state. The nonfunctional Remember me control was removed.

Every available customer product now has one consistent Select action leading to configuration. Sold-out products expose Details. Product copy uses a column layout with aligned action footers; phone actions retain 48 px targets. Original Jayds category icons and scalable pickup/delivery illustrations remain in use. The supplied ZUS layouts inform the flow without reproducing their art, customer information or promotions.

The blank delivery map was an ESM worker loading failure in MapLibre 6. The build and development hooks now copy the installed worker, its sibling module and licence to the public worker directory. The picker supplies that URL before creating the map. Live read-only checks showed successful street tiles, fonts and labels with zero worker/runtime errors. The dialog is centered at 1024/1920 px, fills portrait widths 360/390/430 px, provides 48 px controls, separates search/map/address entry and keeps manual coordinates under a fallback disclosure. Empty/invalid points and points outside delivery coverage disable selection. On LAN HTTP, GPS explains its secure-connection requirement; pin placement and search remain available.

An explicit server-only origin allowlist fixes checkout requests through the Wi-Fi address while retaining authentication and rejecting unrelated origins. After the laptop resumed on a different network, the configured preview origin was updated to `http://192.168.23.21:4000`. A read-only legal endpoint check returned the expected 401 login requirement instead of the origin rejection. The previously denied live legal-acceptance POST was never executed; acceptance behavior was exercised with isolated fixtures.

The real-worker regression uncovered Serwist's default automatic reload on an online event, which could cancel a cached product navigation or erase an unsubmitted form. `reloadOnOnline` is disabled; existing providers refresh data and app updates remain explicit. Saved public menu links use document navigation offline, while private pages, APIs and Flight responses remain network-only. The offline notice now occupies normal page space instead of covering the Back control. Initial guest authentication no longer remounts the navigation tree unnecessarily; account changes still remount private page forms.

Verification: 96 backend/security tests passed; all three application builds passed, with the customer rebuilt after the final offline fixes. Workspace typecheck and focused lint passed. Customer browser fixtures covered 55 products at seven phone/tablet/desktop widths, synthetic login and refresh, legal acknowledgement, logout cleanup, options, stock conflicts, reorder and lost-response recovery with one canonical sale. POS/administrator browser operations and motion checks passed. All 52 published live products opened over LAN HTTP with zero runtime errors or failed responses. Map layout, invalid coordinates, delivery coverage and HTTP GPS explanation passed on five widths. The real service worker passed public-only cache inspection (103 resources), offline reload, product selection, bag addition, return navigation, retained drafts and reconnection preserving the same document and unsaved notes.

Formal Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy and Notice publish 18 numbered sections each, preserve the former published version and distinguish contract agreement, privacy acknowledgement and optional permissions. The additive live legal migration preserved profile/order/product/stock row counts and hashes, immutable acknowledgement protections and restricted grants. See `customer-legal-review.md` for primary sources and the remaining business/counsel review requirements.

These checks do not certify every possible defect or measure field performance. Real Android/iOS installation, device push permissions and live payment/courier transactions were not performed. HTTPS remains required for GPS, installation and device notifications on the Wi-Fi origin. Legal publication is a formal draft grounded in the implemented service, not attorney review or regulatory certification. No live sales, courier bookings or provider-payment operations were created during QA. Current customer preview: `http://192.168.23.21:4000/menu`.

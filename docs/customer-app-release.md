Implemented behavior:

- The phone/tablet shell retains Jayds’ branding, the original mascot logo and welcome promotional banner. Startup has a brief logo/banner sequence with a bounded session fallback. Home has a published-product carousel with manual controls and pausable autoplay; reduced-motion preferences stop autoplay and suppress transitions. Page/container fades retain fixed checkout controls and the existing draft/provider lifetimes. Static brand images use direct public URLs so the service worker can display them offline.
- Scheduled pickup offers today and tomorrow in Asia/Manila, in complete 15-minute windows, with at least 20 minutes’ notice and Maya prepayment. The default preparation lead is 10 minutes; Admin can change it while keeping it shorter than the minimum notice. Slot order capacity, item-unit capacity and eligible physical stock are reserved atomically. Incoming unreceived stock is excluded, and reserved batches must remain eligible through the pickup window end.
- Unpaid holds expire after 30 minutes or at preparation due time, whichever comes first. Cancellation and expiry release unused holds once. A late verified payment stays recorded for manual refund review without reopening a cancelled order. Stock is consumed only when staff starts preparation. POS separates future scheduled orders, and KDS shows orders only after their actual preparation/ready transition.
- Checkout validates current server prices and settings, preserves canonical retry behavior, and protects account/guest order access. Existing order statuses, URLs, recipes and historical records remain compatible. Delivery has a map/manual address flow and requests GPS after a user action; branch coordinates and the initial 8 km radius remain operator-verifiable settings.
- Customers can save addresses and favorite products, acknowledge versioned legal terms, submit private support requests, and subscribe to order alerts. Admin customer care includes checked support responses, uncertain provider references and failed-push counts. Failed operations refreshes show an explicit retry state and retain the last loaded data with a warning. Reconciliation associates an existing merchant-dashboard checkout/order only after remote reference checks; it does not initiate a charge, booking or refund.
- The PWA caches public menu pages and assets. Private/authenticated pages, APIs, orders and payment requests use the network. Offline ordering/payment submission is unavailable. Push uses a private outbox with leases, bounded retries and authorization/state checks before delivery.
